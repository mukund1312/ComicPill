ComicPill privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

ComicPill helps you decide what comic to read next and what to buy. This page explains, in plain language, what the app does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your phone, there is no account, and there is no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your library, reading history, ratings, playlists, reading journeys, wallet, settings, and profile (name, username, bio and photo) are stored only on your device. ComicPill has no servers and no user accounts, so we never receive this information. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Reading your own comic files

You can open comic files (CBZ, CBR, CB7, ZIP, PDF and EPUB) that are already on your phone. ComicPill uses Android's system file picker, so it needs no storage permission and only sees the files you choose. Chosen files are copied into the app's private storage on your phone, and your reading progress, bookmarks, folders and collections are saved there too. None of it is uploaded anywhere. Removing a comic from the reader, or uninstalling the app, deletes the copy. Reading in incognito mode saves no progress at all.

Permissions, and why

  • Camera — to take a profile photo and to photograph your comic shelf for scanning. Photos are used only when you take them.
  • Photos — to choose a profile picture or screenshots of a digital library. Only the images you pick are read.
  • Notifications — to send the daily reading reminder, if you turn it on. It is scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent from a server.

You can change any of these at any time in your phone's Settings.

Optional AI features (your own key)

If you choose to add your own API key from Anthropic (Claude) or OpenAI in Settings → AI assistant, then only when you tap "Build my journey" or "Scan", the text you typed or the photos you selected are sent directly from your phone to that provider, using your key, to be read. They do not pass through ComicPill, and we never see them. The provider's own terms and privacy policy apply to that data. For OpenAI, ComicPill asks that the request not be stored.

Your key is kept in your phone's secure storage (the system Keychain / Keystore). It is never included in the data export and is sent only to the provider it belongs to. You can remove it at any time. If you never add a key, these features stay off and nothing is sent anywhere.

Checking for updates

On Android builds installed directly (not from the Play Store), the app can check once a day whether a newer version exists by downloading a small public file from GitHub Pages (mukund1312.github.io/ComicPill/version.json). No personal information is sent, but like any website visit, GitHub can see your IP address and that a request was made. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & data.

Sharing a playlist

When you share a playlist, ComicPill creates a link that contains the playlist's name and the titles in it. Anyone who has the link can see them. The link opens a page hosted on GitHub Pages. Nothing is shared unless you choose to send the link.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • No selling or sharing of your data — we do not have it.
  • No account or sign-in.

Your control

Settings → Privacy & data lets you export a copy of your data and clear your reading data. Settings → AI assistant lets you remove your key. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the device.

Children

ComicPill is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it. Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.
